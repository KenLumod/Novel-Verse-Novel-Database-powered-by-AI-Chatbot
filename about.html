About NovelVerse

AI-powered novel discovery, built for readers who love web fiction.

Our Purpose

NovelVerse is an AI-powered novel discovery platform designed to help readers find their next favorite web novel. Instead of manually scrolling through hundreds of titles, you can describe the kind of story you want and let our AI assistant do the searching for you.

How the AI Works

Our chatbot analyzes your description — themes, tropes, character types, plot elements — and searches through our novel database to find the most relevant matches. It ranks results by how closely they match your request and presents the top recommendations with explanations.

Our Collection

We curate novels from popular web fiction platforms. Each entry includes detailed descriptions, genre tags, author information, ratings, and cover art — giving you everything you need to decide if a novel is right for you.

Technology

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The AI recommendation engine uses natural language processing techniques to match user queries with novel descriptions, including keyword extraction, TF-IDF scoring, and semantic similarity.

How to Use NovelVerse

1

Open the AI Chat

The AI Assistant lives on the left side of every page. It's always ready to help you discover novels.

2

Describe What You Want

Type a description of the kind of story you're looking for. Be as specific or general as you like.

3

Get Recommendations

The AI searches our database and returns the top 5 matching novels, with explanations for each match.

4

Explore & Read

Click on any novel card to see its full details, or browse the library to discover more stories.
